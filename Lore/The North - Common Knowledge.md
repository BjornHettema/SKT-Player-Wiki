# The North - Common Knowledge

*A starter page in the DM's own words - edit freely.*

- The **North** is the wild region of the Sword Coast north of Waterdeep: great cities like **Waterdeep**, **Neverwinter** and **Silverymoon**, with long stretches of frontier, forest and mountain between them.
- Roads such as the **High Road** and the **Long Road** link the towns. Off the roads, travellers meet orcs, barbarian tribes, monsters and worse.
- Lately, travellers bring strange stories: **giants** have been seen far from their mountains, and more often than anyone can remember.
