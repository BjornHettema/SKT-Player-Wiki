# Welcome to Storm King's Thunder

*Read before session 1. A PDF version of this page is in Discord.*

## What kind of game is this?
We're playing a long campaign of **Dungeons & Dragons** (5th edition, 2014 rules) set in **the North**: the wild, cold frontier of the Forgotten Realms. Big cities like Waterdeep and Neverwinter sit along the coast, and between them lie forests, hills, mountains and long lonely roads. You start as beginners (level 1) and grow into heroes. Lately, travellers have been telling strange stories about *giants* seen far from their mountains. Probably just stories.

We play online: **Discord** for voice and chat, **Tabletop Simulator** for maps and miniatures.

## What is a "sandbox"?
Most video games lead you down one path. A sandbox doesn't: it's a living world, and **you** decide where to go and what to chase.

- **At the start, the road is clear.** You'll have an obvious goal in front of you, like session 1: get to Nightstone and see about those goblins.
- **Later, the map opens up.** You'll pick up rumours, favours and enemies, then choose which ones to follow. The world keeps moving whether you act or not.

What makes it easier:
- **There is no single right path.** If it sounds interesting to your character, it's a good choice.
- **Keep a list of leads.** See [[Leads]]. At the end of each session we'll decide together what you want to do next time.
- **Not every fight is meant to be fought.** Some dangers are far stronger than you. Talking, sneaking, bargaining and running away are all real options.
- **Ask questions.** "What do I see?", "Who lives here?", "Can I try...?"

## Where your story begins
In Waterdeep, each of you came across this notice:

> **REWARD**
> Goblins from the hills have been troubling the village of **Nightstone**. **Lady Velrosa Nandar**, High Steward of Nightstone, offers a fair reward to capable adventurers willing to deal with them.
> Nightstone lies ten miles north of the High Road, between Waterdeep and Daggerford. Follow the signpost, and ask for Lady Nandar at the keep.

Each of you answered it, for your own reasons. You set off south together along the High Road and have been walking for a few days. **Session 1 begins on the last evening of the journey, around a campfire beside the road.**

## What everyone knows
- **Waterdeep**, the City of Splendors: the greatest city of the North, and where you met.
- **The High Road** runs along the coast between Waterdeep and Daggerford. It's busy by day and lonely by night.
- **Nightstone** is a small walled village on a river, about ten miles off the road. It's ruled by the Nandars, a noble family from Waterdeep, and is known for hunting trips and a cosy inn run by a dwarf.
- The **Ardeep Forest** lies just north of Nightstone. Elves live there, and they keep to themselves.

## Our table rules (short version)
Full version: [[Table Charter]].
- **Rules as written.** D&D 5e (2014 rules), no house rules.
- **Death is permanent.** If your character dies, you make a new one.
- **Only roll when the DM asks.** Roll in #dice-rolls or in Tabletop Simulator.
- **Levelling** happens at story milestones, during a short or long rest.
- **Inspiration:** the DM hands it out for great play. Please remind the DM!
- **Alignment is strictly enforced.**
- **Secrets** are encouraged. Message the DM privately if you like, but no secret messages between players: say it at the table, in character.
- **No metagaming.** Your character only knows what your character has seen or heard.
- **One absence** is allowed, with the DM's OK in advance.

## Tips for new players
- **Say what your character wants to do.** The DM tells you when to roll.
- **Know five numbers on your sheet:** Armour Class, hit points, your main attack bonus and damage, and your best skill.
- **In combat, your turn is:** move, one action, and sometimes a bonus action. Plan while others take their turns.
- **Work together.** Say your plan out loud.
- **Failing is part of the fun.**
